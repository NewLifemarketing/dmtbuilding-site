The DMT Story

With more than 50 years of combined experience, DMT Building Group brings craftsmanship, precision, and innovation to every build.

Our team lives and breathes Insulated Concrete Form construction, delivering foundations and full structural systems that stand the test of time. From the first footing to the final floor assembly, we take pride in being a true turnkey contractor, handling every stage with skill and relentless attention to detail.

What sets us apart is simple: an uncompromising commitment to quality and customer satisfaction. Whether it's an addition, a custom home, a cottage retreat, or a multi-storey build, we combine technical expertise with a hands-on approach that ensures every project runs smoothly, safely, and to the highest standard.

We don't just build structures — we build trust, reliability, and results that last generations. At the heart of it all is our promise: smart builds for real life.

What Sets Us Apart

True turnkey delivery

From consultation and quoting through footings, structure and handover — one contractor, one point of accountability.

ICF specialism

We don't dabble in insulated concrete — it's our core trade, on every foundation, wall and structure we build.

Bedrock expertise

Footings scribed and pinned directly onto exposed Canadian Shield rock — precision work few crews will even attempt.

Craftsmanship, measured

Every lift braced, checked and poured to spec. The below-grade work nobody sees gets the same care as the finishes everybody does.

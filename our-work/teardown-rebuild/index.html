Project Story

Teardown & Rebuild

An urban infill teardown: old structure out, new engineered footings and foundation in — on a tight lot between existing homes.

  1. Home
  2. Our Work
  3. Teardown & Rebuild

Service: New Builds

The Challenge

Infill work means neighbours a few metres away on both sides, tight machine access, spoil that has to leave the site, and a new foundation that has to respect existing grades and drainage around it.

What We Did

After demolition, we excavated and formed the complete new footing and foundation package within the existing streetscape — sequenced tightly so the site spent as little time open as possible.

On the Site

The Result

A clean, engineered base for the new home, delivered without drama on a lot where there was no room for any.

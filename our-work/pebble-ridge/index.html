Pebble Ridge

A large engineered footing and foundation package — heavy reinforcement, deep pads and disciplined layout at subdivision scale.

The Challenge

Scale. A footprint this size means long runs of formwork, heavy rebar schedules, engineered pad footings and grade beams — all of which have to land exactly where the drawings say, because everything above depends on it.

What We Did

We laid out and formed the full footing package: continuous strip footings, deep reinforced pads and dowelled connections, poured in sequence and checked against the engineering at every stage.

The Result

A foundation package delivered flat, square and to spec — the kind of below-grade work that makes every following trade's job easier.

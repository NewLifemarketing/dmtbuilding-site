Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

DMT Building Group ("we", "us") operates dmtbuilding.ca. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you submit a quote or contact form we collect the information you provide: name, email, phone number, project address, project details, and any files you upload. Our website host and form provider may also collect standard technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and analytics.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes, schedule consultations and deliver our services. If you consent to SMS updates, we may text you about your request; reply STOP at any time to opt out. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

Form submissions are processed through our CRM provider (GoHighLevel) and stored securely. Map features are provided by Google Maps, which may set its own cookies subject to Google's privacy policy.

Retention & your rights

We keep enquiry information as long as needed to serve you and meet legal obligations. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting info@dmtbuilding.ca or calling 705-333-3329.

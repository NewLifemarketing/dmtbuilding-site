Before You Ask

What happens after I submit the form?

We review your project details and get back to you to book a consultation — usually a call first, then a site walk if the project's a fit.

What should I include in my request?

Whatever you have: the property address, drawings or sketches, photos of the site (especially any exposed rock), and a rough sense of timing. More detail means a faster, sharper quote.

Is the quote really free?

Yes — consultation and quote are free, with no obligation.

I don't have drawings yet. Can we still talk?

Absolutely. We can work with you and your designer from the earliest stage — often saving money by flagging site realities before the drawings are finished.

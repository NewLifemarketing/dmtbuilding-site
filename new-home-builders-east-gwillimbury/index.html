Which new home are you looking for?

If you searched for new home builders East Gwillimbury, there is a fair chance you want a house in one of the new subdivisions: pick a lot, pick a model, close on a date. We are not that kind of builder, and it is better to say so in the first paragraph than the last. DMT Building Group builds custom homes in insulated concrete form (ICF), one at a time, from the footings to the roofline, and builds footing and foundation packages for other builders.

The Town of East Gwillimbury treats these as different jobs too, and it is worth seeing how. A house going up in a subdivision the Town has not yet taken over carries one set of paperwork. A house going up on its own lot, especially a rural one without municipal water and sewer, carries a completely different one. Both are new homes in East Gwillimbury. They do not share many of the same questions.

Our office is in Barrie, Ontario. East Gwillimbury is one of the Greater Toronto Area towns on the service area we publish, and our own crews do the site prep, footings, foundation and ICF walls.

How East Gwillimbury's paperwork splits the two

The left column is East Gwillimbury's Acknowledgement of Unassumed Property, a form an owner signs to proceed with work in a subdivision the Town has not yet assumed without the builder's consent. It says the builder or developer remains responsible for sidewalks, curbs, streetlights and grading until assumption, and lists the risks plainly: losing the grading deposit held by the builder, having to remove work to correct grading, losing the Tarion warranty through unauthorized alterations, and paying for damage to municipal infrastructure. That is the world of the East Gwillimbury subdivision builder. The right column is ours.

Building on your own lot in East Gwillimbury

A one-off house in East Gwillimbury, outside the new subdivisions, meets four Town requirements that a subdivision buyer never sees.

The farm next door

Minimum Distance Separation, an Ontario formula the Town applies, is required for a proposed dwelling within 1,500 metres of any building used for livestock. The MDS I calculation forms part of a complete building permit application.

The septic system

East Gwillimbury's building permits cover installing or altering a private sewage system. A new house without sewer needs one, designed and installed as its own permitted work.

Inspections near water

The Province requires septic systems within 100 metres of the Lake Simcoe shoreline or watercourses in its watershed, or near a York Region drinking water well, to be inspected every five years.

Wellhead protection

East Gwillimbury lists six well sites with Wellhead Protection Areas, including on Mount Albert Road, Queensville Sideroad, Centre Street and Leslie Street.

The septic system also shapes the site plan more than people expect. East Gwillimbury's own maintenance advice says not to drive or park on a leaching bed, not to build a deck or driveway over the tank, and not to change the property's drainage. On a new build, that means the bed, the tank, the driveway and the excavation for the house all get placed together, before the first footing is laid out. It is one of the reasons we walk a rural East Gwillimbury lot before we quote it.

The development charges, and the part that can wait

East Gwillimbury collects development charges for itself, for York Region and for the two school boards, and they are calculated when the building permit is issued. The Town's rate schedule effective July 1, 2026 lists $153,353 for a single or semi-detached dwelling: $59,701 Town, $84,718 York Region and $8,934 for the school boards.

The part that matters for a rural lot is in East Gwillimbury's deferral policy, approved by Council and effective April 15, 2025. The Town says it would be premature to charge the water and wastewater components of its development charges on land that has no municipal water or sewer. On qualifying unserviced land, those components are deferred until the property connects, with payment due within 60 days of servicing being made available. The policy covers the Town's own charges, not area-specific ones; on the July 2026 schedule that is $8,112. It does not apply to land that is serviced where the owner would simply prefer not to connect, and a Deferral Acknowledgement has to be signed before the permit is issued. Confirm the current figures and your eligibility with the Town for your address.

What East Gwillimbury's permit process looks like

Each step is from the Town's own Building Permits page.

  1. 1

    Check the zoning

    Zoning By-law 2018-043 sets setbacks, size and height. A minor variance takes two to three months; a rezoning, four to six.

  2. 2

    Pre-screening

    Submissions are pre-screened for completeness. The application date is the date pre-screening is approved, not the date you sent it.

  3. 3

    Other agencies

    LSRCA for environmentally sensitive areas, the MTO near highway corridors, and a tree permit where it applies.

  4. 4

    Build and inspect

    Permit card and approved plans on site. Inspectors attend within 48 hours of a request, weekends and holidays excluded.

East Gwillimbury is also partway through a Zoning By-law Conformity Update to bring the by-law in line with new legislation and its proposed Official Plan, so check whether anything affecting your lot is changing before the design is final.

New Home Builders East Gwillimbury — FAQs

Do you sell homes in East Gwillimbury subdivisions?

No. We build custom homes in ICF on individual lots, and we build footings and foundations for builders. For a house in a new subdivision, the subdivision's builder is who you buy from.

What does "unassumed" mean for a new East Gwillimbury home?

The Town has not yet accepted responsibility for the subdivision's sidewalks, curbs, streetlights and grading, so the builder or developer still holds them. The Town warns that changes made without the builder's consent can cost the grading deposit or the Tarion warranty.

Is there a farm setback for a new house in East Gwillimbury?

Yes. A Minimum Distance Separation calculation is required for a proposed dwelling within 1,500 metres of any building used for livestock, and it forms part of a complete building permit application.

Are development charges lower on a lot without town water and sewer?

Under the Town's deferral policy, the water and wastewater components of its own development charges are deferred on qualifying unserviced land until the property connects. Other components are paid as usual.

How quickly are building inspections done?

The Town says staff attend within 48 hours of a request, excluding weekends and holidays.

Are you based in East Gwillimbury?

No. Our office is in Barrie, and East Gwillimbury is one of the Greater Toronto Area towns on our published service area.

Overview

A new build is the one chance to get everything right from the ground up — and that is exactly where DMT Building Group lives. We build custom homes in insulated concrete form (ICF) from the footings to the roofline, so the structure, insulation, vapour protection and framing go up as one continuous system.

Whether it's a family home in Barrie, a walkout on a sloped lot, or a lakefront build on solid rock, we self-perform our scope: site prep, footings, foundation, walls and floor assemblies, coordinated with our network of trusted local trades and suppliers.

Why ICF for New Builds

A continuous thermal barrier

ICF walls deliver an effective R-22 to R-26 with no gaps, studs or cold spots — lower energy bills from day one.

Concrete-core strength

A reinforced concrete core stands up to wind, weather and time in a way conventional framing can't match.

Quiet and comfortable

The mass of concrete plus continuous foam means a noticeably quieter, draft-free home year-round.

Built for generations

Less maintenance, no rot, no shifting — a structure designed to outlast its mortgage.

New Builds — FAQs

How long does an ICF new build take?

ICF walls typically go up faster than block or poured-and-framed equivalents because structure, insulation and framing happen in one step. Overall schedule depends on design, permits and site conditions — we lay out a realistic timeline in your quote.

Can I use my own house plans?

Yes. Most conventional plans convert cleanly to ICF. We review your drawings, flag anything that needs an engineer's input, and coordinate the changes before construction starts.

Is an ICF home more expensive to build?

Wall-for-wall, ICF usually carries a modest upfront premium over conventional framing — offset by lower heating and cooling costs, faster wall assembly and less long-term maintenance. Every project is quoted individually, free.

Do you build outside Barrie?

Yes. We build anywhere within about two hours of our Barrie office — Simcoe County, Muskoka, Georgian Bay, Orillia, Huntsville, Bracebridge, Port Carling, Rosseau and down into the Greater Toronto Area.

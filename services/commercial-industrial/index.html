Overview

The same properties that make ICF exceptional for homes — concrete strength, continuous insulation, fast wall cycles — make it a serious contender for commercial and industrial buildings, where energy costs and durability hit the bottom line directly.

DMT brings residential-grade care to commercial-scale work: engineered footings and foundations, full-height ICF wall systems, and the coordination muscle of a specialist contractor backed by a deep local partner network of ready-mix, pumping, haulage and supply companies. Recent commercial work includes the layout, framing and custom interior build-outs for an indoor sports facility in Owen Sound — proof the same precision carries well beyond the foundation.

Why ICF for Commercial & Industrial Work

Operating costs that stay down

Continuous R-22+ insulation on a commercial footprint compounds into serious HVAC savings, year after year.

Durability where it's abused

Concrete walls shrug off the impacts, humidity and wear that destroy framed and steel-skin assemblies.

Fire and sound performance

Concrete assemblies deliver superior fire resistance ratings and acoustic separation.

Speed on the critical path

Structure, insulation and the fastening surface in one wall cycle keeps commercial schedules moving.

Commercial & Industrial ICF — FAQs

Is ICF code-approved for commercial buildings?

Yes — ICF is a recognized concrete forming system under the Ontario Building Code, used in commercial, institutional and industrial construction with standard engineering.

What size projects do you take on?

From footing-and-foundation packages for other GCs to complete structures. Send drawings for a free assessment — we'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit.

Can you work under a general contractor?

Yes. We regularly deliver the concrete/ICF scope for builders and GCs, and we're equally comfortable carrying the concrete scope from footings through to the walls.

Why choose ICF over tilt-up or block?

ICF delivers the concrete structure and the continuous insulation in one step, with no separate insulation trade — often the difference on both schedule and lifetime operating cost.

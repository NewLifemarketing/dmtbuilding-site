Overview

ICF isn't just a foundation product. Stacked storey over storey, it produces full-height concrete buildings with continuous insulation from footing to roofline — and DMT builds them regularly.

Multi-storey ICF demands real discipline: wall alignment, bracing, engineered floor connections and clean concrete lifts on every level. With 50+ years of combined experience, our crews treat every lift like the finished product depends on it — because it does.

Why ICF for Multi-Storey Construction

Storey-over-storey strength

Reinforced concrete walls carry multi-storey loads with ease and give the whole building rigidity framing can't.

Fire and sound separation

Concrete walls dramatically outperform framed assemblies for both — a huge win between floors and units.

Whole-building efficiency

The continuous insulation scales: every added storey enjoys the same R-22+ performance.

Faster wall cycles

Each floor's structure, insulation and fastening surface go up in one operation, keeping the schedule tight.

Multi-Storey Construction — FAQs

How many storeys can you build in ICF?

ICF systems are engineered for multi-storey use — residential projects of two and three storeys are routine, and taller structures are possible with engineering.

How do floors connect to ICF walls?

Floor systems hang from engineered ledger and hanger connections cast into or fastened to the concrete core — specified per project by the engineer.

Is multi-storey ICF cost-effective?

The economics improve as you stack: one wall system replaces structure, insulation, vapour barrier and strapping on every floor, and energy savings compound with size.

Can you do commercial multi-storey?

Yes — see our Commercial & Industrial ICF service, or send us your drawings for a free assessment.

Plain English

Most of the people who call us are home builders who already know the product. But if you are not in the trade, here is the translation — call it whatever you like, we will know what you mean.

“Styrofoam blocks” / “foam forms”ICF — insulated concrete forms.
“Concrete house”Usually conventional forming, which is a different thing from ICF.
“Poured foundation”Same thing we call it.
“Basement walls”Same thing we call it.
“Energy efficient home”Same thing we call it.
“Soundproof house”Same thing we call it.
“Hurricane or tornado proof”Same thing we call it.

The one thing people get wrong: The thing people get wrong most often is thinking you cannot put drywall right on ICF. You can.

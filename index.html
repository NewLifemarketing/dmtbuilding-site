Why ICF — and Why DMT

Insulated Concrete Forms wrap a reinforced concrete core in continuous insulation, so structure, insulation, vapour protection and framing go up as one system. The result: effective R-22 to R-26 walls with no gaps or cold spots, lower energy bills, and a building that lasts generations.

DMT lives and breathes this method. With 50+ years of combined experience, we're the crew other builders call for the hard part — including our signature specialty, footings scribed directly onto exposed bedrock.

  • Continuous R-22–R-26 insulated walls — no thermal bridging
  • Reinforced concrete strength, quieter rooms, cleaner finishes
  • Our own crews on the concrete scope — one point of accountability
  • Local Simcoe County trust and a deep partner network

How We Work

Before we mobilize we need two things from you: drawings and permits. From there, here is the sequence a job actually moves through on site, in our words.

  1. 1

    Footing pads and piers

    Layout, then footings, pads and piers formed to the site. On a typical job we form roughly 100 linear feet a day, plus pouring and then stripping the forms.

  2. 2

    Weeper and interior stone

    Drainage goes in and the interior is brought up with stone — the groundwork that keeps the finished space dry.

  3. 3

    ICF foundation / frost wall

    Insulated concrete walls go up: braced, aligned, poured and checked at every lift.

  4. 4

    Waterproofing

    Waterproofing goes on the exterior — done once, done right, without the extras that get oversold.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about ICF construction — from the crew that builds it every day.

Can you put drywall directly on ICF?

Yes. When installed by trained professionals, drywall can be applied right onto ICF walls, even in foundation areas. ICF combines insulation, vapour protection, and built-in framing, so there is no need for extra studs or layers. This means a cleaner finish, less waste, and a wall system that is strong, efficient, and ready to showcase quality workmanship.

Are ICF homes more energy efficient?

Absolutely. ICF walls create a continuous thermal barrier that keeps heat in during the winter and out during the summer. Unlike conventional framing, there are no gaps or cold spots, which means lower energy bills and a more comfortable home year-round.

How long does ICF construction last compared to traditional builds?

ICF structures are built to stand the test of time. The reinforced concrete core provides unmatched strength, while the insulation protects against moisture, shifting temperatures, and wear. This creates homes and cottages that last for generations with less maintenance.

What is the R-value of ICF compared to conventional forming?

ICF walls typically deliver an effective R-value of R-22 to R-26, depending on the block used. More importantly, the continuous insulation and airtight design outperform traditional framed walls of the same rating — conventional assemblies leave weak spots at every stud and joint.

Where does DMT Building Group work?

We're based in Barrie, Ontario and build within about a two-hour drive — Simcoe County, Muskoka, Georgian Bay, Orillia, Huntsville, Bracebridge, Port Carling, Rosseau and down into the GTA. If you're not sure whether your site is in range, ask; the quote is free either way.

What part of the build do you actually handle?

The concrete and ICF scope, self-performed by our own crews: footings, pads and piers; weeper and interior stone; ICF foundations and frost walls; waterproofing; above-grade ICF walls; slabs and floor assemblies; and structural framing in smaller scopes. We work alongside your builder and trades rather than replacing them.

What does ICF really cost against wood frame?

Not as much more as people think, once you take everything into consideration — the framing, insulating and vapour work you are not paying for, and the energy bills afterward.

Is ICF slower to build?

No. ICF is faster.

What is the honest downside of ICF?

Nothing other than the upfront cost. That is the trade-off, and we will say so on the phone.
